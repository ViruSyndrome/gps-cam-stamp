Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary

GPS Cam Stamp stamps photos in your browser. Your photo files are not uploaded to our servers for processing. Approximate location coordinates are sent to third-party services you opt into by using address lookup, map thumbnails, and weather. We also use analytics, advertising, and error monitoring tools that may set cookies or collect device/usage data.

Photos and Images

Photos you take or upload are processed in your browser with the HTML5 Canvas API. We do not operate a photo-upload server for stamping. Stamped images download from your browser to your device. Optional EXIF GPS writing (when enabled) runs locally via a client-side library (piexifjs from a CDN); coordinates are written into the downloaded JPEG on your device, not uploaded to our servers.

If you use My Photos, stamped images and related metadata (label, timestamp, coordinates) can be stored locally in your browser via IndexedDB. That data stays on your device until you delete it. To clear it: open the My Photos tab and delete saved items, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings (site data / cookies and site data for gpscamstamp.com).

Location Data

Your device may provide GPS (or network-based) coordinates through the browser Geolocation API. Those coordinates are held in page memory to build the visible stamp. They are not stored on GPS Cam Stamp servers.

Depending on features you enable, approximate coordinates are sent to:

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — reverse geocoding (address from coordinates). See the OSMF privacy policy.
  • Esri ArcGIS World Imagery — map tile requests that include a tile corresponding to your approximate location. Esri receives the tile request (and typical HTTP metadata). Attribution (“Powered by Esri”) is shown on map thumbnails.
  • Open-Meteo — weather for the coordinates you stamp. See Open-Meteo terms.

Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

We use Google Analytics to measure traffic and engagement. Google may set cookies and process IP/device data under Google’s policies.

We may display Google AdSense ads. AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies for ads and measurement. See Google’s advertising and privacy documentation for details and opt-out options.

EEA/UK: A Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) must also be enabled in the AdSense console for visitors in those regions; site-level Consent Mode defaults are not a substitute.

We do not claim that this site uses no cookies. Third-party scripts above may set cookies or local storage.

Error Monitoring (Sentry)

We use Sentry for client-side error reporting so we can fix bugs. Sentry may receive technical diagnostics (for example error messages, browser type, and page URL). Session replay sampling is disabled on this site. See Sentry’s privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — address lookup (receives lat/lng)
  • Esri World Imagery — map tiles (receives tile requests for approx. location)
  • Open-Meteo — weather (receives lat/lng)
  • Google Analytics — site analytics
  • Google AdSense — advertisements
  • Sentry — error monitoring

What the stamp is (and is not)

The stamp is a visible location/time label drawn onto the image. It is not cryptographic proof of authenticity and can be edited like any image. Do not treat it as tamper-proof evidence by itself.

Children's Privacy

GPS Cam Stamp is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
