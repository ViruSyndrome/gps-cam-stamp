| **Full Description** | GPS Cam Stamp is a free online GPS photo stamper that runs entirely in your browser. Capture a photo or upload existing images, then stamp them with GPS coordinates, street address, altitude, date and time, optional map thumbnail, compass heading, and weather. Five stamp templates (Classic, Minimal, Pro, Card, Enterprise) and batch upload for multiple photos. Built as a free desktop and mobile alternative to paid GPS camera apps.
